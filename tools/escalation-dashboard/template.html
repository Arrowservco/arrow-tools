    Agents: escalated % of their transfers

    Mix-adj. index = escalated ÷ what you’d expect given the agent’s call types (1.00 = typical). Flagged = at least 2 standard errors above expected and index ≥ 1.25, so small samples and naturally high-escalation call types don’t get blamed on the agent.
    Definitions & notes
    • Escalated transfer = a transfer whose destination skill name contains “Escalation” (e.g. EMA_Credit_Escalation, CTE_Credit_Escalation, NHE_Res_Resolution_Escalation).
    • Escalated % of transfers = escalated transfers ÷ all of that agent’s (or call type’s) transfers in the data.
    • Rows are transfers, not unique calls; a call transferred more than once counts each time.
    • Expected escalations = for each of the agent’s transfers, the overall escalation rate of that full skill, summed. Standard error assumes independent transfers.
