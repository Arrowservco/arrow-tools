Semester Scheduler

Fill in the questions, then build the schedule. Clinics take a whole day; classes share a day up to the limit you set. You can click any cell in the finished schedule to edit it before you export.

Calendar

Which days of the week are school days?

Semesters, classes and clinics

Set how many weeks each semester runs, then list its required classes and clinics with the number of sessions each needs (classes are usually 15, sometimes 20).
