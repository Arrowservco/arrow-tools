Semester Scheduler

Answer the questions and it lays out each semester week by week, in the same layout as the RAD2805 program-hours workbook: one sheet per semester, up to 3 lines per day, and class, lab and clinic hours with running totals. Click any line in the grid to change it before you download.

STEP 1

Program calendar

School days
One per line: a date or a range with “to”, then the label that should show in the grid. Nothing else is scheduled on these days.
STEP 2

Semesters

For each semester: how many weeks, how many clinic days (numbered Clinic 1, Clinic 2…) and which weekdays they fall on, then the required classes. Each class meets on its day every week as RAD 101 U1, U2… until all its units are done; leftover units are filled in on open days near the end.

STEP 3

Build and download

Rebuilding replaces any changes you typed into the grid.
